## Permitted use

- **Personal and educational viewing.** Content may be viewed and referenced for non-commercial purposes.
- **Limited quotation.** Small portions may be quoted with proper attribution to the author.

## Prohibited use

- **Full reproduction.** Redistributing or replicating complete projects without permission.
- **Commercial use.** Commercial exploitation without written authorization.
- **Misrepresentation.** Claiming authorship or ownership of this work.
